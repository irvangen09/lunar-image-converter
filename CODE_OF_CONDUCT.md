# Code of Conduct

This project is open to everyone who wants to use it, report a problem or improve it. To keep it a place where that stays possible, everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests and discussions agrees to the following.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Be patient, especially with newcomers and with people who write in a language that is not their first.
- Stay on topic and be specific: say what you did, what you expected and what happened.
- Accept that this is a personal project. A maintainer may decline a change or a request without long debate.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about a person's background, identity or beliefs.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, or advertising unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who do not follow these rules.

To report a problem, use the private form described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and begin the title with "Conduct". Reports are treated privately.

## Scope

This applies to the project's GitHub repository: issues, pull requests, comments and any other space the project uses.
